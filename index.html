About

Henrique Miguel is a designer, writer, and researcher working across visual identity, interactive media, and critical design practice. Based between Chicago and Porto.

His work explores the intersection of graphic design, computational media, and cultural criticism — examining how platforms, algorithms, and interfaces shape visual communication and human experience.
