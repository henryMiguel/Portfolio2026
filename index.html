About

Henrique Miguel (b. 1997, São Paulo) is a designer, researcher, and writer whose practice spans graphic design, moving image, sound, and computational media. His work examines how technological systems shape the production and circulation of visual culture, with a particular interest in the relationships between design, computation, and social behavior. He is a research assistant at the Institute of Art, Design and Society (i2ADS) and is completing an MFA in Design at the Faculty of Fine Arts, University of Porto (FBAUP).

His research and creative practice have explored topics such as design friction, data visualization, artificial intelligence, and the formation of digital behaviors. Alongside his design practice, he works with sound and moving image through independent projects and collaborations between Chicago and Porto.
